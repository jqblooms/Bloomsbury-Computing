Commands

Moving the robot

walk_forward()
move forward one space
turn_left()
turn 90 degrees left
turn_right()
turn 90 degrees right
jump()
jump up or down a ledge while moving forward
light()
switch a tile on or off; light every tile to win

For loops

Repeats a fixed number of times, counted by i.

for i in range(4):
    walk_forward()
    light()

While loops

Repeats as long as the condition holds. Increase i yourself, or the loop never ends.

i = 0
while i < 3:
    jump()
    i += 1
