Shifts and Circulates

Each one moves the bits one place. Watch what happens to the bit at the end.

LCIRC-1every bit moves 1 left; the end bit goes round to the right

RCIRC-1every bit moves 1 right; the end bit goes round to the left

LSHIFT-1every bit moves 1 left; the end bit falls off and a 0 comes in on the right

RSHIFT-1every bit moves 1 right; the end bit falls off and a 0 comes in on the left
