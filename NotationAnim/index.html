Prefix, Infix and Postfix

Change an infix sum into postfix and prefix. Then work each one out with a stack.

Infix to Postfixbrackets round every operation, move each operator to the end, remove the brackets

Infix to Prefixbrackets round every operation, move each operator to the front, remove the brackets

Work Out Postfixread left to right; the first value popped goes on the right

Work Out Prefixread right to left; the first value popped goes on the left
