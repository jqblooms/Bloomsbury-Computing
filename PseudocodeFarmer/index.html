📖 Pseudocode cheat sheet
Plant("Carrot")Plants a seed you own where your cursor is standing.
Buy("Carrot Seeds")Buys one seed packet, 20% cheaper than the shop button. The price never goes up from buying, so a machine can buy, plant and sell the same vegetable forever.
Sell()Sells a fully grown plant where your cursor is standing, for 1.5× what the on-screen Sell button pays.
Has_Crop("Carrot")TRUE if the square has that crop growing. Leave the crop name out to check whether any crop is growing there.
Crop_On_Tile()Which crop is on the square your cursor is on ("Carrot"), or "None" if it's empty. E.g. IF Crop_On_Tile() = "Carrot" THEN ...
Money()How many coins you have right now. E.g. WHILE Money() < 50 DO.
Seed_Count("Carrot")How many packets of that seed you own. E.g. IF Seed_Count("Potato") = 0 THEN Buy("Potato Seeds").
Move_up()Moves your cursor one tile up the field.
Move_down()Moves your cursor one tile down the field.
Move_left()Moves your cursor one tile left.
Move_right()Moves your cursor one tile right.
Move_forward()Moves one tile in the direction your cursor is currently facing.
Turn_left()Turns your cursor 90° left, without moving it.
Turn_right()Turns your cursor 90° right, without moving it.
Set_position(2, -1)Jumps straight to a tile, counted from home: 2 right, 1 up. Home is (0,0) - the tile you were on when you pressed Run, or the tile a machine was placed on.
OUTPUT "hi"Prints a message to the console below.
FOR Index <- 1 TO 5
  ...
NEXT Index		Repeats the lines in between, 5 times. No "DO" on a FOR line.
IF X = 1 THEN
  ...
ELSEIF X = 2 THEN
  ...
ELSE
  ...
ENDIF		Runs the first branch with a true condition. ELSE runs if no earlier condition is true.
WHILE X < 10 DO
  ...
ENDWHILE		Repeats while the condition is true. Compare with =, <>, <, >, <=, >= and combine with AND, OR, NOT. Either side can be maths too, e.g. WHILE N < Total + 1 DO.
X <- X + 1Math uses +, -, *, /, DIV and MOD, with brackets to group. You need this to change a variable inside a WHILE loop, or it runs forever.

A little white dot on your cursor (or a machine's blue diamond) shows which way it's facing.

New vegetables unlock as your total coins earned (top right, not your current balance) goes up. Prices rise fast, so keep selling to afford the next one. Spending money doesn't undo that progress.

Every machine starts at Level 1 and performs one working instruction per second. Upgrade it through Levels 2 to 9 for the matching number of instructions per second. Level 10 performs 15 instructions per second.

IF, ELSEIF, ELSE, ENDIF, FOR, NEXT, WHILE and ENDWHILE control which instruction runs. These control lines do not use an instruction slot.

Click a placed machine to select it. Its cursor turns pink and grows. From there you can edit its code, upgrade it, or sell it back for half the total amount spent on it.

Out of room? Hover over a dim tile at the edge of your garden and click it to buy more land. Each extra tile costs more than the last, and crops take longer to grow the more expensive they are.
